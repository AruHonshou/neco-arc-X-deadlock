Neco Arc Hideout Portraits

Replaces the Deadlock hideout portrait atlas textures with a 30-image Neco Arc set.
No models, materials, UI icons, or audio are changed.

Install with Deadlock Mod Manager, or copy the included VPK to:
Deadlock/game/citadel/addons/

Restart Deadlock after installing or updating the VPK. Remove the VPK to restore the
original hideout portraits. The three texture files are compiled at the original
atlas dimensions and BC7 format used by the game.
